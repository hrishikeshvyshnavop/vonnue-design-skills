### Content
- **Description**: This is a bold, editorial, two-color footer for a creative workspace and event venue. The whole section is one flat field of vivid orange (#FF5900) with all type, the rule and the logo in pure black (#000000); there are no other colors, images, shadows or gradients. The top band carries a giant serif wordmark split across the width: 'Set' at the far left and 'Space' at the far right, with a wide empty gap between them (Quadrant Text 10vw = 144px at 1440, weight 400, line height 115%; cap height ~105px; the 'p' descender drops ~24px below the baseline). Below a tall stretch of empty orange, a 1px black rule spans the full width of the section. Under the rule is an Acknowledgement of Country in the same serif (19.5px, regular, line height 120% = 23.4px): 'Set Space acknowledges the Traditional Custodians of the land on which we work and live, the Wurundjeri Woi / Wurrung peoples of the Eastern Kulin Nation. We recognise their continuing connection to land, water and / community and celebrate their enduring presence and knowledge.' Below it are three columns of small uppercase monospace text (GT Pressura Mono 13px, regular, letter-spacing ~1px, line height 140%, 6.5px between items): 'ROADMAP', 'TERMS', 'PRIVACY', 'MEDIA', 'WALKTHROUGH' in the first column; 'HEY@SET.SPACE', 'INSTAGRAM', 'LINKEDIN' in the second; and the address 'BALAM BALAM PLACE', '15 PHOENIX ST', 'BRUNSWICK VIC 3056' in the third. At the bottom left is the copyright line '© 2026 SET SPACE, ALL RIGHTS RESERVED' in the same mono, and at the bottom right the brand's pin logo (~17.5 × 26px, black): a five-pointed star head with a round hole in the middle, sitting on a long thin stem. Hover: unknown (not visible in screenshot). Overall, the style is loud, typographic and minimal: one saturated color, a huge classic serif and quiet technical monospace details.
- **Layout**: The layout is a single full-width footer, 1440px wide, with a minimum height of 390px + 20vw (678px at 1440). It is a vertical flex column with space-between and a 26px gap, so all extra height sits between the wordmark and the rule. The top band has 19.5px padding; 'Set' starts at x ~2% and 'Space' ends at x ~98%, vertically centered in the band; the two words never move closer, and the empty middle grows with the width. The details block below has the 1px black top border running edge to edge, 19.5px padding and 26px between its three rows. Rows use a four-column grid (equal columns, 13px column gap, 353.5px pitch at 1440): the paragraph spans columns 1–3 (~1047px), so it wraps to exactly three lines; the link band fills columns 1–3 at x 19.5, 373 and 726.5px, with the fourth column empty; the last row has the copyright across columns 1–3 and the logo right-aligned in column 4, both bottom-aligned, ~20px above the section's bottom edge. At 950px and below the grid drops to two columns; at 550px and below it is one column and the side padding shrinks to 6.5px. Keywords: split wordmark, full-bleed color, hairline rule, monospace link grid, acknowledgement of country
- **Functions**: Users can reach secondary pages from the first column: 'Roadmap', 'Terms', 'Privacy', 'Media' and 'Walkthrough'. They can email the team at 'hey@set.space' and follow the space on 'Instagram' and 'LinkedIn'. The third column gives the physical address, Balam Balam Place, 15 Phoenix St, Brunswick VIC 3056, so visitors can find the venue. The acknowledgement paragraph recognises the Wurundjeri Woi Wurrung peoples as Traditional Custodians, and the giant wordmark closes the page with the brand name. Overall, the interface supports brand recall, contact, social following and directions to the physical space.
