### Content
- **Description**: The screens present a corporate sales contact page designed to facilitate enterprise team communication and service requests. The layout is split mainly into two vertical sections: the left side with a headline, supporting text, and contact reasons, and the right side with a detailed contact form. The left side uses a dark-gradient background with a smooth blend of deep blue (#1f1667) fading into near black (#0c0a1a), giving a modern and professional look. Text on the left is white (#ffffff) with a large bold sans-serif header (about 32-36px, weight 700, left aligned), medium subtitle text beneath it (16-18px, weight 400), and a bullet list with blue circular check icons (#4a5aff) and white text to highlight contact reasons. The right side has a clean white (#ffffff) box form with subtle gray text input labels and placeholders (#666666), input fields with light gray borders (#cccccc), and consistent spacing and alignment. Dropdown fields for company size and country feature arrow indicators. The primary call-to-action button is rectangular with medium blue (#3b5aff), white bold text, and changes shade slightly on hover with a shadow or brightness effect. Beneath the form is a small legal disclaimer text about terms and privacy with embedded links styled as blue underlined text. Below the main section, a black background (#000000) section displays a horizontal logo wall of partner company logos in monochrome white for visual trust. Further down, three columns present key benefits (Security, Scalability, Success) in minimalist cards with headings in white (#ffffff), descriptive text in gray (#bbbbbb), and small custom icons above each heading. The typography for headings is bold, around 20-24px, and body text is lighter weight, 14-16px. Next is a testimonial or video section with a central large video thumbnail featuring a translucent dark overlay and a bright blue (#4466ff) play button overlay, captioned by a white headline and subheading. Finally, a dark footer spanning full width includes multi-column navigation links in light gray (#999999) text, each column grouped by category with headings in white and smaller sized fonts. The entire page uses a modern sans-serif font (like Inter or Helvetica Neue), with consistent vertical and horizontal spacing, generous padding around elements, and high contrast for readability. The overall style is corporate, professional, and trust-inspiring with a clean UI and clear visual hierarchy.
- **Layout**: The page employs a two-column main layout at the top: left side for headline, text, and bullet points; right side for the contact form. Below is a full-width logo wall spanning horizontally with evenly spaced logos. Next, a three-column section contains three content cards aligned horizontally with equal widths and spacing. Below that, a full-bleed video section centered with text above it. The footer uses a multi-column grid structure with four to five narrow vertical columns, each containing grouped navigation links with consistent gaps and padding. Vertical spacing between sections is generous. Horizontal alignment is predominantly left for text except for centered elements in video section and footer social icons. The layout uses clear grouping of content chunks, consistent gutters, and a visual hierarchy emphasizing the form with whitespace.
- **Functions**: Users visiting this interface can perform specific tasks such as reading introductory and trust-building content about the enterprise sales team and solutions. Next, users can fill in a detailed contact form including personal information (first name, last name), business-related data (business email, phone number, company, company size, job title, country) and free text explaining their needs or questions. The input fields and dropdowns enable precise data entry, while the clearly styled 'Contact Sales' button submits the form. The form provides validation cues by requiring all fields and linking to legal disclaimers for terms acceptance. Below the form, users can learn about trusted partners via logos to build confidence. Additional informative sections highlight security, scalability, and success benefits, supporting decision-making. A testimonial or introduction video can be played by clicking the visible play button to better understand the product value. The footer provides quick links to company, product, solutions, social media, and support resources, facilitating navigation to further information or actions such as support or product comparison. Combined, these elements facilitate the broader goal of enabling enterprise prospects to connect with the sales team efficiently, gather relevant information, and take action to request demos, learn pricing, or start discussions, ensuring a seamless journey from interest to engagement.
